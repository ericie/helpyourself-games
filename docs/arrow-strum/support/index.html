Arrow Strum support

Found a bug, or just want to say hello? Write to me at support@helpyourself.games.

I’m stuck on a level

Tap Hint to see an arrow you can free.

I can’t hear anything

Arrow Strum follows your iPhone’s Ring/Silent switch. Check that first, then Sounds in Settings.

Where is my progress kept?

Only on your iPhone. If you delete the game, your progress goes with it, and I have no copy to restore.

Can I start over?

Yes. Choose Reset progress in Settings.
