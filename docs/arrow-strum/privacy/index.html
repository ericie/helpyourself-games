Arrow Strum privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

Arrow Strum keeps your progress, best times and settings on your device only. The game itself never sends anything anywhere.

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. The game has no accounts, no built-in analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use cookies or any third-party data services.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous totals such as downloads, how often the game is opened and crash reports. Apple collects this, not the game, and it never identifies you to us. You can turn it off at any time under Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

If you write to us by email, we use your message only to reply to you, and we do not add you to any list.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
